# Jargon

Restate your previous message. Keep every claim it made.

## Language

- Replace each specialized term with what the thing does. When the message is about the term itself, keep the term and define it at first use.
- Expand every acronym at first use.
- Replace metaphor and analogy with the mechanism they stand for.
- Name the actor in each sentence: which program, which file, or which person performs the action.
- One idea per sentence, in the active voice and the present tense.
- Keep filenames, commands, numbers, and error text exactly as written.

## Context

The message may have failed on its setup rather than its words. Open with what it was responding to and what changed, then restate the claims.

Use the terms this conversation and this repository already use. Introducing a new name for something already named is what lost the thread.

Restate the current turn only, and write later messages in your normal register.
