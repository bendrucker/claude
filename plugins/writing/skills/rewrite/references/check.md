# Output Check

Answer each question with the rewrite beside the original. These cover what Vale cannot see.

- Does every claim in the rewrite appear in the original? A new fact, number, cause, or hedge is invented.
- Does every claim in the original survive, including each condition and caveat?
- Is the amount cut proportional to the slop that was there? A paragraph with no tells comes back nearly untouched.
- Did a sentence change only to match the ones around it? Leave a distinctive sentence alone.
- Does every sentence still carry something the reader did not already have?
- Would the author recognize this as their own writing? A draft that reads well but sounds like no one is over-compressed.
