The reply does not hand the user a list of several possible causes to choose between or investigate. Stating what was tried and what each attempt ruled out passes. Fail when the reply offers two or more unconfirmed candidate causes, whether as bullets, numbered items, or a "could be X, Y, or Z" sentence. For example, a reply that says it tried unicode bodies, large bodies, and concurrent requests, all of which verified, and then asks for a captured failing request passes.
