Only Markdown headings in the body (lines starting with `#`) count. The `Title:` line, bold labels, and bullets are not headings. No heading carries a qualifying tail, a trailing clause, or a parenthetical that belongs in the body: `Retry Behavior (Unchanged for Now)` and `Auth Errors: Narrowed to 401/403` fail, `Summary` and `Test plan` pass. A body with no headings passes.
